## Platform

web

## Users

Agencies and client-services teams managing multiple client projects at once. Their job is coordinating deliverables, tasks, and stakeholders across clients and keeping the whole team aligned in one place.

## Product Purpose

A team collaboration app for agency/client work. It exists to give teams one place to coordinate projects, tasks, conversations, and files instead of switching between separate tools. Success means a team can see and move all client work forward from a single workspace.

## Positioning

An all-in-one hub: the core claim is consolidation. Tasks, chat, and files live together so work and its context stay connected. A neighboring single-purpose tool (tasks-only or chat-only) could not truthfully make that claim.

## Operating Context

Agency work involves many concurrent client projects, deliverables, and internal/external stakeholders. The first surface is the working app UI — a dashboard covering projects, tasks, team members, and messaging — extending the existing scaffold.

## Product Principles

1. Consolidation first: keep tasks, conversations, and files in one workspace so context never fragments across tools.
2. Built for multi-client agency work: make it easy to see and switch between many client projects, deliverables, and stakeholders.
3. Clarity that enables action: a team should understand the state of client work at a glance and move it forward.
4. Operable product before promotion: the first surface is the working app, not a marketing site.
5. Performance and clarity are explicit quality bars, not afterthoughts.
